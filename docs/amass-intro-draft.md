**Team 13 — FourWells** 👋

We're working the trastuzumab/HER2 track, and our angle is deliberately narrow: with
only four guaranteed wet-lab wells, **the ranking is the science**. Anyone can generate
thousands of candidates by mid-afternoon. We're spending our time on which four to send
and being able to say why.

Where AMASS fits for us: our epitope axis rests on a published claim that is easy to
get wrong — that affinity does *not* imply epitope. A HER2 binder can have picomolar
affinity on a conformational epitope nowhere near trastuzumab's site, which scores
nothing on a competitive-binding assay. We used literature search to pin that down to
specific citations rather than asserting it from memory, and it changed how we score de
novo designs: no structural evidence of domain IV contact means the axis is capped, not
guessed.

Happy to compare notes with anyone else ranking under a hard well budget rather than
optimising a single number.
