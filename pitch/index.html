How we decide

Four decisions we can defend.

  • Epitope is near-free in optimisation and fatal in de novo. So every de novo design is conditioned on HER2 domain IV, and one with no structural evidence it gets there is capped, not guessed.
  • Stability is the beatable axis — so it gets the real effort.
  • We rank on the joint probability, not a weighted sum. Excellent on two axes and hopeless on the third scores zero S-tier. An average hides precisely that; a product cannot.
  • Never four slots on four variants of one parent. Slots are allocated across clusters, so four wells buy four hypotheses.

A 524,346-row training set loses to a string comparison.
Why, measurably: the FACS label itself only predicts a tighter KD at AUC 0.608 (n = 27 molecules carrying both). The ceiling is the assay gap, not the model — so no model fit to these labels can clear it. The pool is 114 after removing 48 too similar to the parent to be submittable, 17 training leaks, 8 parent replicates and 3 non-10-mers — every exclusion removes designs that would have flattered us. Our two worst calls: YNAGMYVYTG ranked 1 of 114 and measures 124 nM; WKGYGSYVHD binds at 0.723 nM and ranked 112 of 114.

What is next

The ranking layer outlives this challenge.

  • Every design ships with a kill criterion, so the lab result is informative either way — we wrote down what would falsify each one before seeing any data.
  • The ranking layer is generation-agnostic. Gates, axes and portfolio read the record, not the antigen — we rehearsed it blind on two targets it had never seen.
  • What we did not do: try to out-model Foundry in seven hours.
Placing today earns 12, 8 or 4 extra wells on top of the guaranteed four.

A good pitch is not separate from the lab result. It buys more shots at it.
Honest limit: generation does not yet retarget — the reference antibody stays trastuzumab whatever target you pass. The ranking layer retargets; the generator does not. See docs/blind-rehearsal.md.
